Escalade Starlight Headliner: The Full Build Breakdown

A1 Customs PA · Updated August 2026 · 5 min read

The Cadillac Escalade might be the single best canvas for a starlight headliner: a huge, flat roof, a cabin built for rear passengers, and an interior that already feels one step from first class. Here's exactly what an Escalade starlight costs, how the install works, and what it looks like done right.

What It Costs

Our large-SUV starlight install — Escalade, Suburban, Denali — is a flat $1,150. Want the full transformation? The large SUV package pairs the starlight roof with full-cabin ambient lighting (doors, dash, console, footwells) from $1,500, installed together in one same-day appointment.

Why Escalades Take More Fibers

That third row means the Escalade's headliner is enormous — nearly double the fiber count of a sedan roof. We hand-place every fiber so the star field stays dense and random from the windshield to the tailgate, with mixed star sizes for depth. Rear passengers get the same sky as the front — which matters if your Escalade works events, livery, or family duty.

The Install, Start To Finish

  1. Morning drop-off — we confirm your star density, colors, and any logo work.
  2. Headliner comes down — carefully, with the right trim tools. No broken clips, no marks.
  3. Fibers go in by hand — hundreds of them, one at a time, routed back to a hidden light engine.
  4. Reassembly and testing — factory fit, every zone cycled through the full color range.
  5. Evening pickup — app walkthrough, then you drive out under your own night sky.

Colors, Effects & Custom Logos

Everything is app-controlled: ice white for the Rolls-Royce look, deep purple like the build above, red, blue, green — plus twinkle and shooting-star effects and music sync. And because each star is placed individually, we can shape them into a logo over the rear cabin. Your brand in your roof.

An Escalade with a starlight roof doesn't feel modified. It feels like Cadillac should have built it that way.
