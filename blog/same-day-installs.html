How We Turn Around Full Lighting Installs In One Day

A1 Customs PA · Updated August 2026 · 5 min read

"Same-day turnaround" isn't a marketing line — it's how our whole shop is built to run. Here's exactly what happens between dropping your car off in the morning and driving out glowing that night, and why the $100 deposit is what makes it all possible.

Why Same-Day Matters

Most shops will keep your car for two or three days for a starlight install. That's two or three days of Ubering to work. We schedule one car per slot, prep everything before you arrive, and finish the job the day it starts. Every lighting package we sell — ambient, starlight, or the full combo — is same-day.

What Your Install Day Looks Like

  1. Morning drop-off. We walk the car together, confirm your colors and layout, and note every panel we'll touch.
  2. Teardown and prep. Trim comes off carefully with the right tools — no pry marks, no broken clips.
  3. The build. Fibers are threaded, diffusers are tucked behind trim lines, and power is tapped cleanly so nothing drains your battery or throws codes.
  4. Reassembly and testing. Every panel back to factory fit. Every zone tested through the full color range.
  5. Evening pickup. We hand you the app, walk through colors, modes, and music sync, and you drive out glowing.

Why We Take A $100 Deposit

Because same-day service only works if the schedule is real. When you book a slot, we've reserved a full day of shop time and prepped materials for your specific vehicle. A no-show doesn't just cost us — it costs the customer who could have had that date.

The policy is simple and fair:

  • A $100 deposit locks in your appointment — and it's applied to your total, so it costs you nothing extra.
  • Life happens. Miss your date and you get one make-up appointment, no questions asked.
  • Miss the second one and the deposit is forfeited.

How To Book

Use the booking page to send us your car and preferred date — it takes about two minutes. We'll text you to confirm, you pay the deposit through a secure link, and your slot is locked. Prefer to talk it through first? Call or text (732) 812-1285 or DM @A1customs_pa.

Drop off in the morning. Drive out under your own stars that night.
