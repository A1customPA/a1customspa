Questions? Answered.

Good To Know

How long does the install take?

One day. Drop your car off in the morning, pick it up glowing that evening — every package we offer is same-day turnaround.

Will it damage my car or void anything?

No. Panels come off with proper trim tools (no pry marks, no broken clips), wiring is hidden and tapped cleanly so it won't drain your battery or throw error codes, and everything goes back to factory fit.

Can I change the colors after?

Yes — you control everything from your phone: millions of colors, brightness, zones, and music-sync modes. We walk you through the app before you drive off.

Can you put a custom logo in my roof?

Yes. Because every star is hand-placed, we can shape them into logos — like the Hellcat roofs in our gallery. Send us your idea and we'll quote it.

Where are you located — and do you travel?

We're based in Pennsylvania and serve PA, New York, and New Jersey. And yes — we travel. Mobile installs add a travel fee based on your location — tell us where you are when you book and we'll quote it upfront, no surprises.

How do I lock in an appointment?

Send a booking request (2 minutes), we confirm your date by text, then a $100 deposit locks it in — and it counts toward your total, so it costs you nothing extra.

What if I miss my appointment?

Life happens — you get one free make-up date, no questions asked. Miss the second one and the deposit is forfeited.
