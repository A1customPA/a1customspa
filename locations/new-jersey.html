Lighting Installs For New Jersey

New Jersey is in our blood — call or text us at (732) 812-1285 and you'll see the area code to prove it. A1 Customs PA installs starlight headliners and full-cabin ambient lighting for drivers across North Jersey, Central Jersey, and down the Shore.

Jersey drivers have two options: bring the car to our Pennsylvania shop for the classic same-day install, or book a mobile install and we'll come to you — driveway, garage, or shop. Mobile jobs add a travel fee based on your town, always quoted upfront before you put a dollar down.
